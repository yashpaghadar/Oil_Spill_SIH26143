Scientific Honesty Feature: Bragg Scattering Physics Gate Autonomous refusal mechanism preventing false accusations under calm-wind sea regimes

The Physics Principle: Sentinel-1 C-band radar relies on ocean capillary waves (1–3 cm wavelength) generated by surface winds to reflect radar pulses back to the antenna (Bragg scattering). When oil is present, it dampens these ripples, creating dark patches.

Why Honest Rejection is Critical: If wind speed drops below 2.0 m/s, the entire sea surface naturally becomes mirror-smooth, generating dark "look-alike" areas without any oil. Naive AI systems falsely accuse innocent ships under low winds. OILED automatically closes the Bragg Gate and refuses attribution when wind is outside the 2–12 m/s window.
